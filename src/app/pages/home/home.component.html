06 • EXAMPLE

How WE CARE helps a new snack stall

Example: an entrepreneur wants to sell fresh fruit drinks at a local event.

PHASE 1

The entrepreneur brings

A product idea, expected customers and an approximate budget.

What they arrived with:
  • Fresh mango & berry smoothie recipe
  • Target: Local school sports day attendees
  • Initial seed budget: $60
PHASE 2 • WE CARE

WE CARE prepares

A brand name, simple banner, price list, promotion plan and sales record.

What we executed:
  • Brand: "Juice Spark" + bright fruit logo
  • Foldable tabletop banner & QR menu board
  • Cost-per-cup matrix ($1.10 cost, $3.50 price)
  • WhatsApp pre-order link & Instagram flyer
PHASE 3 • LAUNCH

The stall opens

The owner serves customers, collects feedback and tracks sales.

Day 1 Outcome:
  • 142 cups sold at event
  • $340 net profit generated
  • 35 repeat customers saved on WhatsApp
  • Documented learnings for next pop-up
